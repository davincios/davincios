About Flymble

What is Flymble?

Flymble is a flight booking platform that helps you travel anywhere affordably. In addition to financing, our powerful search engine compares prices across dozens of different airlines to find you the cheapest rates.

What is required to book with Flymble?

To book with Flymble you need:
  • You must be over 18 in order to pass the credit check.
  • You must be a resident of the UK in order to apply.
  • You must have a UK mobile phone in order to apply.
  • You must have a UK Debit or Credit Card.

Is there any interest associated with Flymble payment plans? Do you charge a fee?

Yes, we do. Flymble charges between 3% and 45% APR interest. The corresponding finance charge is the only fee associated with an Affirm loan - we don’t charge late fees, service fees, prepayment fees, or any other hidden fees. We strive always to be more transparent and fair than any other form of financing.

I can only book flights between £250 and £2000. Why?

Our credit provider currently only allows payment plans for order values ranging from £250 to £2000. The order value equals the total sum of all airfares and fees.

Do I need to pay off all my instalments before I can fly?

No! Simply pay 1/10th upfront and you receive your ticket. Fly now, and pay later.

What are my payment options?

When you make a purchase of £250 or more with Flymble, you can pay over 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, or 10 months. Maximum order values are £2000.

Do I have a credit limit with Flymble?

Unlike a credit card, Flymble is not a revolving line of credit. While customers can take out multiple Flymble payment plans at once, each Flymble loan application is evaluated separately as a closed-end transaction. An application from a returning customer may be denied, however, if that customer has failed to repay other Flymble payment plans on time or if the customer shows excessive borrowing behavior.

What is Fly Now Pay Later and how is it related to Flymble?

Fly Now Pay Later Limited is the credit provider for all Flymble payment plans, and is authorized and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority under registration number 778540.

Does Flymble perform a credit check?

Yes, when you book your first flight with Flymble, we perform a ´soft´ credit check to help verify your identity and determine your eligibility for financing. This ´soft´ credit check will not affect your credit score.

Is my personal information secure with Flymble?

Yes, protecting your personal information is very important to us. We encrypt sensitive data including paspoort data. We also maintain physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect your information. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone. You can read more about our Privacy Policy.

How come Flymble is able to offer Payment Plans?

Flymble works with an independent financing partner to offer short-term loans/credit to travellers.

Booking flights with Flymble

What is an e-ticket?

The e-ticket is your flight confirmation from Kiwi.com which you should print out and bring with you on your trip. After your reservation is complete, we will send you an email confirmation with an attached e-ticket.
Please read your e-ticket carefully because it contains important information about your flights and the check-in process. If your flight is not eligible for online check-in, you will need to show the airline your e-ticket so that they can print out your boarding pass.

What if there is an delay in receiving my e-ticket?

We have already started processing your booking. There may be a delay due to minor technical issues and we will send you confirmation as soon as the booking has been completed
If you have not received any email from us at all, please contact our Customer Support Team to ensure that we have the correct email address for you.

Which name should I use when booking my ticket?

You should use the names exactly as they are written in your passport or travel ID.
Your passport will indicate which names are first names and which names are surnames. Examples: If you have recently been married but your passport still has your maiden name, you should use your maiden name in the booking. If your passport says "John James Smith" but you don't commonly use your middle name, you still need to add it to your booking. Your middle name should be added together with your first name in the same input box. In this example, the first names would be John James. Similarly, all surnames should be added together in the correct order in the surname box. Some airlines will not allow you to board the aircraft if the name on your booking does not match your travel ID or passport. Airlines can charge a fee to correct your name after the booking is made.

How do I know if my tickets are reserved?

As soon as you receive the confirmation email.
You should receive the confirmation email less than 30 minutes after making your booking.

What phone number should I provide?

Please provide us with one or more phone numbers that we can reliably reach you on throughout your travels.
If there are any schedule changes, cancellations, airport strikes, etc, we need to inform you and let you know your options. Entering a phone number Select your country code. Add your phone number in the international format. For example, if you are from the UK, you should remove the first zero from your number.

What If I didn't receive a confirmation e-mail of my flight tickets?

Please check your spam folder and then contact us if you do not find anything.
You should receive an email confirmation for your flights within two hours of making your booking, otherwise: It could be in your spam folder There could have been a typo in your email address when you created the booking, so please contact us on chat or by phone to correct this for you
